Useful idea

Try not to repeat the same weakness.

If your team stacks several Pokémon that are weak to the same type, one opponent can sweep too easily. Filtering helps you compare options and reduce repeated weaknesses.

A balanced team can be more reliable than a team that only looks strong on paper.
