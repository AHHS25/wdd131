Strategy tip

Balance role and type, not only power.

A strong team usually mixes speed, defense, and utility. A trainer who only picks powerful attackers can struggle against poor type matchups.

Start with one fast attacker, one bulky defender, one support option, and then add type coverage to reduce major weaknesses.
